# 0082. The reference scenes gate at native 1440p

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Status:** Accepted (the user's decision of 2026-11-10; amends
  [ADR-0067](0067-the-game-is-a-city.md)'s decision 4 for Phases 14 and 15;
  CLAUDE.md §10.1, §10.7 and §12 amended)

## Context

ADR-0067 gates Phases 14 and 15 on Bistro, the city and the interior each
holding 144 FPS, a 1%-low of at most 6.94 ms, at native 4K on the RTX 3060 Ti.
Phase 14's pull requests since have won that frame back a few hundredths of a
millisecond at a time. After the resolve and TAA were traced (2026-11-10), the
exact changes left in them came to 0.01 to 0.03 ms; the city's rooftop stood
at 8.80 ms, 1.86 ms over.

The user walked the city in `scene_viewer` on their 2560 × 1440 display, saw
issues there they want to work on instead, and stated on 2026-11-10: "Instead
of applying small incremental changes to get the 6.94ms goal, I want to focus
on a few bigger issues that I saw during my test of the big city reference
scene. Change the gate to be < 6.94 ms at native 1440p on the RTX 3060Ti."

The passes that work per pixel shrink with the pixels (the city street's
resolve 2.04 to 0.92 ms, TAA 0.81 to 0.35, the occlusion 0.90 to 0.40). The
geometry shrinks less: the levels of detail take coarser levels where a pixel
is larger, but the voxel and small-triangle rasters' work falls more slowly
than the pixels (the rooftop's voxel raster 0.79 to 0.65 ms), and culling, the
radiance cache's update and the shadow map's pages barely change.

## Decision

1. **The gates.** At Phase 14's exit Bistro, the city and the interior each
   hold 144 FPS, a 1%-low of at most 6.94 ms, at native 2560 × 1440 on the RTX
   3060 Ti with the phase's features on; at Phase 15's exit they hold it in
   shipping builds. Native keeps §10.1's meaning at that size: every one of
   its 3.7 million pixels resolved each frame, no upscaling, no dynamic
   resolution, no frame generation. ADR-0041's rule stands that no quality is
   given up to meet a number.
2. **The measurement follows.** `render_perf` draws the reference scenes at
   2560 × 1440 by default, so the nightly measures the gate's size;
   `--ref-size=3840x2160` measures 4K by hand. `benchmarks/reference_budgets.txt`
   is re-based at 2560 × 1440 (below), and the guard rail of ADR-0041 (no phase
   ends with a reference scene slower than when it began) reads at that size.
3. **The scene viewer draws the gate's size.** `scene_viewer` draws
   2560 × 1440 by default into a 1280 × 720 window, and F11 switches to
   exclusive fullscreen on the window's monitor at its own mode, so a
   2560 × 1440 display shows the frame pixel for pixel.
4. **What does not change.** §10.1's target, 240 FPS at native 4K for the first
   game during its development, and §10.4's table, its plan, stand as
   ADR-0067's decisions 4 and 5 have them: the phase exits still report each
   §10.4 line against its budget, measured at native 4K. The synthetic scenes
   (`benchmarks/render_budgets.txt`, the `_4k` scenes) stay at 3840 × 2160, as
   do the GPU microbenchmarks. Renderer pull requests state their cost in
   milliseconds and DRAM bytes at the gate's size.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the gate at 4K.** The rooftop is 1.86 ms over it, and the levers
  left inside the image-neutral rule are each a few hundredths of a
  millisecond (2026-11-10's traces); the user's choice is to spend the time on
  the issues they saw instead.
- **Gate at both sizes.** Two gates double the nightly's reference runs and
  leave the 4K one where it was; the 4K numbers stay measurable by hand.

## Consequences

- Every view holds the gate already (the table): the city's rooftop has the
  slowest 1%-low, 6.05 ms, 0.89 ms inside it; the park's is next, 4.66. The work
  moves to what the user saw in the city.
- `benchmarks/reference_budgets.txt` is generated afresh at 2560 × 1440 (every
  entry, the same 1,129) from `reference_scenes-2026-11-10-1440p` and its second
  round. That ends the nightly's two 4K overruns of 2026-11-10, both from pull
  requests the nightly had not yet measured: the rooftop's first meshlet cull,
  0.309 ms against 0.297, which #163's occlusion refinement spends to draw less
  (its rooftop frame 0.74 ms faster), and Bistro hedge's first raster, 0.412 ms
  against 0.409.
- The reference budgets' history at 4K ends at `reference_scenes-2026-11-06`
  and the A/B results in the pull requests before this one; 4K comparisons
  from now on need `--ref-size=3840x2160` on both builds.
- The 240 FPS target at 4K is further from what the gates measure: the
  first game's development starts from a frame measured at 1440p, and its
  gate (ADR-0067's decision 4) has to be checked at 4K when it begins.
